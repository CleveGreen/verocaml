#+TITLE: Recursive and Aggregate Outcome Coverage

This ledger records grouped semantic coverage for aggregate specifications,
recursive aggregates, recursive ranks and specifications, invariant
transitions, and encapsulated mutation.  Ordinary cases use the closed outcome
projection.  Specialist Cram/tool lanes inspect private certificates, complete
lowering shapes, runtime behaviour, solver boundaries, or compiler diagnostics.

This group has no UI golden and no outcome resource maximum.

* Aggregate Recursive Specifications

~positive-recursive-result-matrix~ selects verified unit dispositions, selected
functions, and postcondition obligation kinds across recursive aggregate result
fixtures.  ~tuple-source-project-parity~ compares a ~Single_source~ fixture with
an explicit ~Dune_project~, excluding only named input-mode facts.

The specialist lane checks bounded resource failures, internal VIR routing,
reconstruction and admission boundaries, runtime tuple truth tables, and
zero-solver paths.  Generated identities, complete renders, and private counters
stay in that lane.

* Aggregate Specifications

~positive-aggregate-matrix~ selects verified units and representative functions
for aggregate, variant, model, cyclic, and function specifications.
~frontend-rejection-matrix~ selects stable frontend codes and keyed rejected
units for unsupported specification forms.

The specialist lane checks model authority, malformed raw identities, private
validator boundaries, and lowering structures not represented by ~Outcome~.

* Recursive Aggregates

~construction-and-projection~ selects verified status, its unit, construction
and projection functions, and a postcondition obligation kind.
~frontend-rejection-matrix~ selects stable unsupported-form codes and keyed
units.

The specialist lane checks SMT sort mapping and complete recursive SST/VIR
layout, selector, tag, and bound construction.

* Recursive Invariant Transitions

~verified-transition-matrix~ selects six verified units, their outer functions,
and a transition-preservation invariant kind.  The two counterexample cases
select their unit, owning function, invariant identity, and
~transition-preservation~ boundary.

The specialist lane checks source-protocol failures, lifecycle and teardown,
ordered reconstruction, authority attacks, call affinity, consumed reuse, and
negative vocabulary.

* Recursive Rank

~accepted-rank-matrix~ selects accepted mutable and generic rank units.
~invalid-rank-matrix~ and ~unsupported-type-matrix~ select stable frontend codes
and rejected units.

The specialist lane checks rank certificates, VIR and Logic IR metadata,
profile classification, private authority attacks, generated identities, and
zero-solver rejection paths.

* Recursive Specifications

~verified-recursive-specification-matrix~ selects verified units and functions
for helper, opaque/reveal, higher-order, and structural recursion fixtures.  It
also selects entry-measure and strict-descent obligation kinds where relevant.
~opaque-equation-needs-reveal~ selects counterexample status, its function,
postcondition kind, and unit.

The specialist lane checks ordinary erasure, private helper authority, reveal
admission, complete solver-neutral logic declarations, installed-surface
forgery, structural recursion rendering, and pre-solver measure validation.

* Encapsulated Recursive Mutation

~verified-transition-matrix~ selects verified transition clients and equal-branch
behaviour.  ~unsupported-mutation-matrix~ and ~unsupported-surface-matrix~ select
stable rejection codes and keyed units for unsupported mutation and surface
forms.

The specialist lane checks same-CMT certificates, transition reconstruction,
branch joins, metadata authority, uniqueness compiler behaviour, installed
surface boundaries, and private forgery rejection.
