#+TITLE: Proof Routing, Totality, and VC Outcome Coverage

This document describes grouped outcome coverage for proof activation, proof
body assertions, proof modes, direct totality, VC dispatch, and prepared VC
jobs.  Ordinary cases select status, stable codes, semantic kinds, keyed units,
named facts, process facts, and closed parity.  Specialist checks retain
private routing, scheduler, artifact, compiler, runtime, and resource contracts.

This group has no UI golden and no ordinary resource maximum.

* Proof Activation Routing

Host: ~test/proof_activation_routing/outcome_cases.ml~

- ~reached-proof-fanout~ selects the verified unit, representative proof
  functions, and postcondition or call-precondition kinds.
- ~ordinary-contract-matches~ selects verified functions plus local-assertion
  and postcondition kinds.
- ~external-finite-formal-rejected~ selects process exit ~2~,
  ~VERO_DEPENDENCY~, and registered colour forwarding.
- The missing, sibling, later, cross-callable, and opaque reveal cases select
  counterexample status, the failing function, keyed unit, and postcondition or
  call-precondition kind.

The specialist lane checks activation issuance and consumption, path routing,
manifest authentication, rejection before dispatch, and exact private resource
transitions.

* Proof Body Assertions

Host: ~test/proof_body_assertions/outcome_cases.ml~

Reconstruction and parity cases:

- ~immutable-reconstruction-source-cmt-parity~
- ~immutable-reconstruction-matrix~
- ~immutable-reconstruction-cohabitation~
- ~builtin-assert-source-cmt-parity~
- ~builtin-assert-noassert-semantic-parity~

These cases select verified or counterexample status, keyed units, named
functions, local-assertion or postcondition kinds, and declared input or
compile-mode parity.

Proof-region and fact-flow cases include:

- ~exec-proof-region-fact-export~ and the isolated ~exec-region-*~ cases
- ~proof-local-assertion-matrix~ and ~finite-empty-node-assertion~
- ~branch-local-fact-scoping~ and ~one-predecessor-does-not-prove-join~
- ~recursive-proof-summary~ and ~removed-recursive-summary~
- the reveal-placement cases and ~simple-local-assertion~
- ~builtin-assert-false~

Nullary and finite controls include ~finite-empty-node-wrong~,
~non-nullary-ground-abstains~, ~nullary-recursive-symbolic-positive~,
~nullary-recursive-wrong-result~, ~nullary-recursive-reachable-false~, and
~nullary-recursive-symbolic-expected~.  They select the stable verified,
counterexample, or inconclusive status and the owning local-assertion fact.

Frontend and process cases cover raw, effectful, imported, non-boolean,
value-position, mutation, and lookalike assertions.  They select the stable
~VERO_*~ code when available; compiler and semantic-validation failures that
produce no closed verifier outcome remain specialist exceptions.

The specialist lane covers retained PPX carriers, internal reconstruction
shape, forced-ground and retry behaviour, path caches, malformed batches,
session-affine authority, CMT relocation, install and runtime erasure,
authentication attacks, and cleanup.  Complete SST or VIR output, ordinals,
generated paths, and exact counters are not ordinary expectations.

* Proof Mode

Host: ~test/proof_mode/outcome_cases.ml~

- ~proof-declarations-and-region~ selects verified proof declarations and
  assertion, postcondition, and proof-call precondition kinds.
- ~trusted-external-proof-call~ selects the verified ~assume~ and ~caller~
  functions and their postconditions.
- ~recursive-proof-totality~ and ~recursive-bool-proof~ select verified units,
  functions, entry and recursive-measure facts, and postconditions.
- ~negative-entry-measure~ and ~non-strict-recursive-measure~ select
  counterexample units and their failing measure kinds.
- Stable-code cases cover value-position proof regions, mutation,
  higher-order use, loops, external calls, recursive polymorphism, and
  recursive external or higher-order calls.

Specialist checks cover handmade SST, PPX authentication, complete recursive
lowering, malformed decrease and proof-call input, erasure, and installed
private surfaces.

* Proof Region Modes

Host: ~test/proof_region_modes/outcome_cases.ml~

- ~trusted-unique-return~ and ~checked-unique-return~ select verified units,
  functions, postconditions, and proof-call preconditions.
- ~completed-result-invariant-transition~ and ~recursive-control~ select
  verified transition and recursion facts.
- Source-written, old direct-carrier, and
  ~effectful-proof-region-payload~ cases select frontend rejection and their
  stable codes.

Specialist checks cover mode capture and remapping, retained and ordinary
erasure, CMT and Typedtree mutation, validation-stage boundaries, finite
receipts, lifecycle counters, and trace ordering.

* Direct Totality

Host: ~test/direct_totality/outcome_cases.ml~

- ~countdown-totality~ selects the verified unit and entry, arithmetic, call,
  recursive nonnegative, strict-descent, and postcondition kinds.
- ~overflowing-measure-counterexample~ and ~harmless-let-rec~ select their
  counterexample or verified units and measure facts.
- ~mutual-recursion-rejected~ and ~loop-rejected~ select frontend rejection,
  ~VERO_UNSUPPORTED_MUTUAL_RECURSION~ or ~VERO_UNSUPPORTED_LOOP~, and keyed
  units.

Specialist checks cover SCC and descriptor architecture, installed private
surfaces, and malformed termination preparation that has no closed projection.

* Function VC Dispatch

Host: ~test/function_vc_dispatch/outcome_cases.ml~

~serial-threaded-semantic-parity~ selects a verified Dune fixture, keyed
functions and local assertions, explicit execution-mode facts, and closed
serial/threaded parity.

The specialist lane checks frontier construction, bounded concurrency,
scheduler lifecycle, lazy SST, cleanup, retained recursive dependencies,
injected-error ordering, source topology, and private interfaces.

* Prepared VC Jobs

Host: ~test/prepared_vc_jobs/outcome_process_cases.ml~

The cases ~threads-zero~, ~threads-negative~, ~threads-nonnumeric~,
~threads-overflow~, ~threads-duplicate~, ~threads-missing-value~, and
~threads-runtime-maximum~ select exit ~2~, ~VERO_CLI~, and registered
~OCAML_COLOR~ forwarding.

Specialist checks cover the prepared-job bridge, opaque ownership, controlled
resource exhaustion, recursive scheduling and cutoff, telemetry lifecycle,
session cleanup, and private interfaces.
