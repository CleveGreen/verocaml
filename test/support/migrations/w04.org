#+TITLE: Finite Values, Ranks, and Structural Induction Outcome Coverage

This document describes grouped outcome coverage for finite-formal flow,
finite-result promotion, rank domains, and structural induction.  Ordinary
cases select status, stable frontend codes, semantic kinds, keyed units, and
named functions or obligations.  Specialist checks retain private authority,
compiler-artifact, runtime, and bounded-resource contracts.

This group has no UI golden.  Complete SST or VIR renders, generated identities,
ordering, spans, and exact private counters are not ordinary expectations.

* Finite Formal Propagation

Host: ~test/finite_formal_propagation/outcome_cases.ml~

- ~accepted-finite-formal-flows~ selects verified units and representative
  executable, proof, mode, generic, and recursive-descent facts.
- ~generic-policy-rejections~ selects frontend rejection, keyed units, and
  ~VERO_UNSUPPORTED_POLYMORPHISM~,
  ~VERO_UNSUPPORTED_HIGHER_ORDER_FUNCTION~, or ~VERO_UNSUPPORTED_TYPE~.
- ~public-interface-success~ and ~public-interface-rejections~ cover public
  proof functions, unit disposition, and stable aggregate or polymorphism
  codes.
- ~same-cmt-signature-rejections~ selects
  ~VERO_UNSUPPORTED_STRUCTURE_ITEM~ for the supported projected boundary.
- ~retained-provider-consumer~ selects provider and consumer units,
  representative functions, and call semantics.
- ~transferred-precondition-failure~ selects the counterexample unit,
  ~caller~, ~checked~, and ~Call_precondition(checked)~.

Specialist checks cover registry lifecycle, retained ABI and parser attacks,
transfer counters, adjacent-interface authentication, CMI-only input,
unprojected malformed-SST failures, PPX marker syntax, compiler output cleanup,
artifact erasure, and complete internal encoding determinism.

* Finite Induction

Host: ~test/finite_induction/outcome_cases.ml~

~accepted-finite-induction~ selects verified finite-induction, ordinary direct
return, and push-front units; representative functions including
~node_eq_refl~ and ~make_n_nodes~; and recursive strict descent.

The specialist lane covers source and install architecture constraints plus
nonfinite construction barriers that fail before a closed outcome can be
projected.

* Finite Result Promotion

Host: ~test/finite_result_promotion/outcome_cases.ml~

- ~promotion-successes~ selects the verified unit set, representative
  construction and consumption functions, and recursive descent.
- ~failed-obligation-inconclusive~ selects an inconclusive postcondition for
  ~make_n_nodes~.
- ~nondecreasing-actual-counterexample~ selects a strict-descent
  counterexample for ~build~.
- ~frontend-rejections~ selects keyed units and the applicable stable codes,
  including ~VERO_MALFORMED_GHOST_CALL~,
  ~VERO_UNSUPPORTED_HIGHER_ORDER_FUNCTION~,
  ~VERO_UNSUPPORTED_TOP_LEVEL_BINDING~, and
  ~VERO_UNSUPPORTED_MUTUAL_RECURSION~.

Specialist checks cover publication lifecycle and counters, malformed-SST
barriers, call-instance authority, mutable or shared carrier rejection, raw
PPX failure, and retained-result import failures without a closed projection.

* Finite Value Receipts

Host: ~test/finite_value_receipts/outcome_cases.ml~

- ~positive-construction-flows~ selects verified construction and local
  executable or tracked chains.
- ~local-result-transfer~ selects the verified ~Box.make~, ~Box.read~, and
  ~run~ path.
- ~direct-cycle-rejection~, ~indirect-cycle-rejection~, and
  ~recursive-factory-rejection~ select frontend status, keyed units, and the
  stable top-level-binding or structure-item code.

Specialist checks cover receipt identity and lifecycle, completed-callee and
finalization counters, injected failed transfer, unavailable-receipt barriers,
and mutable carriers rejected before backend work.

* Parametric Rank Domains

Host: ~test/parametric_rank_domain/outcome_cases.ml~

- ~positive-rank-domains~ selects three verified units, representative rank
  functions, and recursive descent.
- ~retained-provider-consumer~ selects the provider and consumer units and
  representative retained-rank functions.
- The ~negative_*~ cases select frontend rejection, keyed units, and stable
  unsupported-structure, aggregate, type, or dependency codes for abstract,
  cyclic, function, reference, array, mutable, nonuniform, stale, forged,
  rebound, and unknown payload forms.

Specialist checks cover schema construction, clone repeatability, retained
payload confidentiality, malformed mutual-SCC or tuple actuals, and source
concentration constraints.

* Rank-Backed Specifications

Host: ~test/rank_backed_specifications/outcome_cases.ml~

- ~rank-backed-specifications~, ~rank-backed-proof~, and
  ~shape-only-verification~ select verified units, representative functions,
  and proof or recursion facts.
- ~profile-mismatch-rejection~, ~reference-carrier-rejection~,
  ~function-carrier-rejection~, ~object-carrier-rejection~,
  ~foreign-carrier-rejection~, and ~abstract-carrier-rejection~ select keyed
  units and stable frontend codes.
- ~polymorphic-carrier-accepted~ selects its verified unit.
- ~no-finite-alias-rejection~, ~no-finite-rebinding-rejection~,
  ~no-finite-branch-rejection~, ~runtime-cycle-rejection~, and
  ~runtime-indirect-cycle-rejection~ select keyed units and the applicable
  malformed-ghost, top-level-binding, or mutual-recursion code.

Specialist checks cover rank descriptor architecture, mixed proof domains,
installed surface and filesystem isolation, mode forgetting, malformed
validation barriers, replay attacks, and finite-authority barriers.

* Structural Induction Composition

Host: ~test/structural_induction_composition/outcome_cases.ml~

- ~positive-structural-proofs~ selects verified list and tree units,
  representative proof functions, postconditions, and recursive descent.
- ~one-branch-inconclusive~, ~premise-omitted-counterexample~,
  ~early-assertion-inconclusive~, and ~negative-value-counterexample~ select
  status, keyed unit, owning function, and postcondition or assertion kind.

Specialist checks cover rank and fuel resources, summary authority, deliberate
proof mutants, malformed child relations, conditional revisit barriers, and
private replay or cross-profile attacks.
